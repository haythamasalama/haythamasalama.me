## Overview

::project-shot{src="/apps/whatsapp-business-monitor/overview" alt="The overview of two accounts of a fictional store: this month's cost, messages sent, templates, what needs attention, a daily cost chart, the cost per account and a table of the accounts." width="1440" height="900"}

WhatsApp Business Monitor puts your WhatsApp Business accounts on one screen: templates, costs, numbers and settings. It installs with one command on your computer or your server, so your tokens never leave it. I started it in October 2026.

## What it shows

### Every account at a glance

This month's cost and messages, what needs your attention now, such as a rejected or paused template, and the most-sent templates.

::project-shot{src="/apps/whatsapp-business-monitor/account.webp" single alt="An account's home: this month's cost, messages sent, the balance, a daily cost chart, the cost by type, what needs attention and the most-sent templates of the last 30 days." width="1440" height="900"}
One account's home.
::

### Templates

Each template's status and its sends, deliveries and reads over 30 days. Archive, copy or delete several at once.

::project-shot{src="/apps/whatsapp-business-monitor/templates-actions.webp" single alt="The templates tab with several templates selected and the archive, unarchive, copy and delete buttons above the list." width="1440" height="900"}
Templates, with several selected.
::

### Reports and costs

What Meta charged by category, country and number, and how many free service messages are left this month.

::project-shot{src="/apps/whatsapp-business-monitor/reports-costs.webp" single alt="Reports: what was charged, free messages, free replies left, cost over time, and cost by category and country." width="1440" height="1331"}
Costs for the month.
::

The whole dashboard works in Arabic from right to left, in light or dark.

## Privacy

It runs where you choose, behind your own sign-in, and your WhatsApp tokens stay on that machine.
